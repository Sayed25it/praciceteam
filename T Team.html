Kidney Transplant

A kidney transplant team is a multidisciplinary group of healthcare professionals who work together to support patientsbefore during and after a kidney transplant. The team typically includes transplant surgeons nephrologists transplant coordinators nurses social workers dietitians pharmacists and psychologists. Each member plays a vital rolefrom evaluating the patients suitability for transplant and finding a donor to performing the surgery and providing postoperative care and longterm followup to ensure the success of the transplant.




Experience a happy life with good health

Hygiene is all about keeping yourself clean to stay healthy. This means things like washing your hands regularly, brushing your teeth, and showering. Good hygiene helps stop germs from spreading and keeps you feeling good. Maintaining good hygiene is super important for kidney health! Simple things like washing your hands regularly, especially after using the bathroom, helps prevent infections that can harm your kidneys. Also, staying hydrated by drinking enough water helps your kidneys flush out toxins and stay healthy. Keeping clean overall can really make a difference in keeping your kidneys in good shape.
